How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by sending an email to our support team. We do not currently offer in-app deletion, but all requests submitted below are processed promptly.

Send a deletion request to: lastcallfoodteam@gmail.com

Click the address to open a pre-filled email template

Please include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address or phone number
  • The app name: LastCall Restaurant
  • A brief confirmation that you wish to delete your account

What Happens After You Request

  1. We receive your email and send a confirmation reply within 48 hours acknowledging your deletion request.
  2. Your account is deactivated immediately — you will no longer be able to log in or appear on the customer app.
  3. All personal data (profile, food bag images, store details) is permanently deleted within 7 days of your request.
  4. You receive a final confirmation email once deletion is complete.

Data Deleted vs. Retained

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

Data Type Action Reason
Account credentials (email/phone) Deleted No longer needed
Restaurant name, address, hours Deleted No longer needed
Food bag images (Firebase Storage) Deleted No longer needed
Location data (lat/long) Deleted No longer needed
Order history & transaction records Retained 5 years Legal & tax compliance
Crash/diagnostic logs (Crashlytics) Retained 90 days Aggregated, anonymised data
